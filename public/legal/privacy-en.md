Welcome to Frame It, developed by Luca Retattino. Frame It is an arcade game where players act as a virtual sports cameraman, following the action and capturing highlights. The game is intended for entertainment purposes.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the game or its website. The publisher responsible for the processing described here is Luca Retattino, [BUSINESS ADDRESS AND COUNTRY]. Contact: luca.retattino@gmail.com.

## Information We Collect

Frame It does not require you to create an account. The current game does not ask for your name, phone number, postal address, contacts, personal photos, precise location or payment card details. It does not use your device's real camera or microphone: the camera in the game is entirely virtual.

The developer does not operate a player-account database or receive your locally saved scores and highlights. This does not mean that no personal information is ever processed: support emails, website connection data and information handled by any enabled third-party services are described below.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The game stores records and the rare-highlight album locally on your device, using browser local storage in the web version. The album can include images generated by the game, capture dates and ratings; these are not photos taken with your device's camera. Replay data is temporarily held in memory during play.

This information supports game features and is not uploaded to a developer-operated server by the current game. Local records remain until you remove the relevant app or browser data, or your device clears them. Clearing this data can permanently remove saved progress.
